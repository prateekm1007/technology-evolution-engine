ABSTRACT:
The temperature dependence of the Seebeck coefficient of homoepitaxial metal organic vapor phase (MOVPE) grown, silicon doped $β$-Ga$_2$O$_3$ thin films was measured relative to aluminum. For room temperature we found the relative Seebeck coefficient of $S_{β\text{-Ga}_2\text{O}_3\text{-Al}}=(-300\pm20)\;μ$V/K. At high bath temperatures $T>240$ K, the scattering is determined by electron-phonon-interaction. At lower bath temperatures between $T=100$ K and $T=300$ K, an increase in the magnitude of the Seebeck coefficient is explained in the frame of Strattons formula. The influence of the different scattering mechanisms on the magnitude of the Seebeck coefficient is discussed and compared with Hall measurement results.
