Title: Solid-state battery with garnet electrolyte achieving 400 Wh/kg

We demonstrate a solid-state lithium battery using a garnet-type Li7La3Zr2O12 electrolyte that achieves energy density of 400 watt-hours per kilogram. The battery uses a lithium metal anode and a lithium cobalt oxide cathode.

The ionic conductivity follows:
sigma = sigma_0 * exp(-Ea / kT)

where sigma is conductivity, Ea is activation energy, k is Boltzmann constant, T is temperature.

Assumptions:
- The interface resistance between electrolyte and electrodes is negligible.
- The battery operates at room temperature.
- No dendrite formation occurs during cycling.

Limitations:
- The sintering temperature of 1100 degrees limits scalability.
- The garnet electrolyte is sensitive to moisture.
- The manufacturing cost is 5 times higher than conventional batteries.
