Title: Piezoelectric polymer fibers for smart textile energy harvesting

We fabricated piezoelectric polyvinylidene fluoride fibers that generate 0.5 milliwatts per square centimeter from body motion. The fibers are woven into a textile with copper interconnects.

The generated voltage is:
V = g * d * F / A

where V is voltage, g is piezoelectric coefficient, d is thickness, F is force, A is area.

Assumptions:
- The mechanical strain is uniform along the fiber.
- The fiber is in the beta phase.
- The load impedance is matched.

Limitations:
- The power output is insufficient for most electronics.
- The fibers lose piezoelectricity after washing.
- The manufacturing process is not compatible with standard textile equipment.
