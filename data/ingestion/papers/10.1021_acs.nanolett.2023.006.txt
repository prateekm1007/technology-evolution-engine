Title: Nanostructured bismuth telluride for thermoelectric energy harvesting

We synthesized nanostructured bismuth telluride via ball milling and hot pressing that achieves a thermoelectric figure of merit ZT = 1.2 at 400 degrees. The nanostructuring reduces thermal conductivity while maintaining electrical conductivity.

The figure of merit is:
ZT = S^2 * sigma * T / kappa

where S is Seebeck coefficient, sigma is electrical conductivity, T is temperature, kappa is thermal conductivity.

Assumptions:
- The material is isotropic.
- The electrical contacts are ohmic.
- The temperature gradient is uniform.

Limitations:
- The bismuth telluride contains rare elements.
- The material oxidizes above 300 degrees in air.
- The manufacturing process is energy-intensive.
