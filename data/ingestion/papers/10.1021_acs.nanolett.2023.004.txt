Title: Graphene oxide membrane for efficient desalination

We fabricated a graphene oxide membrane on a ceramic support that achieves water permeability of 50 LMH/bar with salt rejection above 99 percent. The membrane comprises stacked graphene oxide sheets with controlled interlayer spacing.

The water flux is given by:
J = A * (dP - dO)

where J is flux, A is permeability coefficient, dP is applied pressure, dO is osmotic pressure.

Assumptions:
- The membrane is defect-free.
- The feed water contains only NaCl.
- The operating temperature is 25 degrees.

Limitations:
- The membrane fouls in the presence of organic matter.
- The graphene oxide coating delaminates under high pressure.
- The manufacturing process is not scalable beyond laboratory scale.
