Title: Biodegradable polymer blend with controlled marine degradation

We report a polylactic acid and polyhydroxybutyrate blend with ceramic nanofiller that degrades in marine environments within 6 months. The blend achieves tensile strength of 50 MPa, comparable to polyethylene terephthalate.

The degradation rate follows:
dM/dt = -k * M

where M is mass, k is degradation rate constant, t is time.

Assumptions:
- The degradation is surface-limited.
- The marine environment is at 25 degrees.
- The ceramic nanofiller does not degrade.

Limitations:
- The manufacturing cost is 3 times higher than PET.
- The material requires controlled humidity storage.
- The ceramic nanofiller may pose environmental risk.
