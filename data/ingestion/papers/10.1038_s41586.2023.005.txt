Title: Amine-functionalized silica for direct air capture of CO2

We report an amine-functionalized mesoporous silica sorbent that achieves CO2 capacity of 2 millimoles per gram at 400 parts per million. The sorbent is regenerated at 90 degrees Celsius with a purge gas.

The adsorption equilibrium follows:
q = q_max * b * C / (1 + b * C)

where q is loading, q_max is maximum loading, b is affinity constant, C is concentration.

Assumptions:
- The sorbent reaches equilibrium with the gas phase.
- The system operates isothermally.
- No competitive adsorption occurs.

Limitations:
- The sorbent degrades after 1000 cycles.
- The regeneration energy is 3 GJ per ton of CO2.
- The amine groups leach under humid conditions.
