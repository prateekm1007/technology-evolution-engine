Title: LED spectral control for vertical farming yield optimization

We demonstrate that LED spectral control in vertical farming increases crop yield by 30 percent compared to white light. The optimal spectrum varies by growth stage and crop type.

The photosynthetic rate is modeled as:
P = P_max * I / (I + K)

where P is photosynthetic rate, P_max is maximum rate, I is light intensity, K is half-saturation constant.

Assumptions:
- The light distribution is uniform across the canopy.
- The nutrient solution is non-limiting.
- The temperature is constant.

Limitations:
- The energy cost of LED lighting is 60 percent of operating cost.
- The results are specific to leafy greens.
- The system requires 200 kWh per kilogram of produce.
