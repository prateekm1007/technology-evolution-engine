TITLE: Reverse osmosis desalination membrane with graphene oxide coating

ABSTRACT:
A reverse osmosis membrane comprising a graphene oxide coating on a polymer substrate for desalination. The membrane includes a ceramic support layer and a polyamide active layer. The graphene oxide coating enhances water permeability while maintaining salt rejection above 99 percent. The manufacturing process involves layer-by-layer deposition of graphene oxide sheets onto the polymer substrate. A pump pressurizes feed water to 60 bar. The system produces 50 cubic meters of fresh water per day. The membrane requires periodic chemical cleaning to maintain performance.
