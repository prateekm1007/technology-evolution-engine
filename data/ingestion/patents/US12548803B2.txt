ABSTRACT (extracted from patents.google.com):
The present invention relates to a rechargeable battery and a process near zero-energy regeneration of electrodes by recycling spent rechargeable batteries. The present invention relates to a process for near zero-energy regeneration of lithium iron phosphate (LiFePO 4 ) or sodium iron phosphate (NaFePO 4 ) cathode by recycling spent Lithium ferro phosphate rechargeable batteries.
