TITLE: Thermoelectric generator with nanostructured bismuth telluride

ABSTRACT:
A thermoelectric generator comprising nanostructured bismuth telluride legs for waste heat recovery. The device includes a ceramic substrate with interdigitated copper electrodes. The bismuth telluride is synthesized by ball milling and hot pressing. The manufacturing process requires controlled atmosphere sintering. A sensor monitors the temperature gradient across the device. The generator achieves conversion efficiency of 8 percent at a temperature difference of 200 degrees. The polymer encapsulation protects the thermoelectric elements from oxidation.
