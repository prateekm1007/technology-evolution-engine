TITLE: Atmospheric water harvesting system using metal-organic framework sorbent

ABSTRACT:
An atmospheric water harvesting device uses a metal-organic framework sorbent material to capture water vapor from ambient air. The device includes a condensation chamber where desorbed water vapor condenses on a cooled copper surface. A pump circulates coolant through the condensation chamber to maintain surface temperature below the dew point. The sorbent is regenerated by heating with a solar thermal collector. The system achieves water production rates of 0.2 liters per kilogram of sorbent per day at 20 percent relative humidity. The manufacturing process involves synthesis of the metal-organic framework at elevated temperature.
