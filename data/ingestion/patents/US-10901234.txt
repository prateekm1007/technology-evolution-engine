TITLE: Smart textile with integrated piezoelectric sensor and energy harvesting

ABSTRACT:
A smart textile comprising a woven fabric with integrated piezoelectric polymer fibers for energy harvesting and sensing. The fabric includes a flexible circuit substrate with copper interconnects. The manufacturing process involves weaving piezoelectric polyvinylidene fluoride fibers with cotton yarn. A sensor monitors strain and motion. The system generates 0.5 milliwatts per square centimeter from body motion. The polymer coating protects the electronics from washing. The textile maintains functionality after 100 wash cycles.
