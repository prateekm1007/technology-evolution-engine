TITLE: Photoelectrochemical cell for artificial photosynthesis

ABSTRACT:
A photoelectrochemical cell for artificial photosynthesis comprising a semiconductor photoanode and a metal cathode. The photoanode comprises titanium dioxide nanotubes coated with a cobalt phosphate catalyst. The cathode comprises a platinum-coated carbon paper. The manufacturing process involves anodization of titanium foil followed by electrodeposition of the catalyst. A pump circulates electrolyte through the cell chamber. The system achieves solar-to-hydrogen efficiency of 2 percent. The ceramic membrane separates the photoanode and cathode compartments.
