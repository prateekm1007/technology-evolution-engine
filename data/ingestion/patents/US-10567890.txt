TITLE: Direct air capture sorbent using amine-functionalized porous silica

ABSTRACT:
A direct air capture system using amine-functionalized porous silica sorbent for CO2 removal. The system includes a fixed bed adsorber where ambient air passes through the sorbent at atmospheric pressure. A heat exchanger provides thermal energy for sorbent regeneration at 90 degrees Celsius. The manufacturing process involves grafting amine groups onto mesoporous silica. A pump circulates regeneration gas through the bed. The sorbent achieves CO2 capacity of 2 millimoles per gram at 400 parts per million CO2 concentration. The ceramic support provides structural stability.
