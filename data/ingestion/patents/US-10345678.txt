TITLE: Solid-state lithium battery with garnet electrolyte

ABSTRACT:
A solid-state lithium battery incorporating a garnet-type electrolyte is disclosed. The battery comprises a lithium metal anode, a garnet electrolyte layer, and a cathode comprising lithium cobalt oxide. The garnet electrolyte has the composition Li7La3Zr2O12 with aluminum substitution. The manufacturing process involves tape casting of the electrolyte layer followed by sintering at 1100 degrees. A sensor monitors cell temperature during operation. The battery achieves energy density of 400 watt-hours per kilogram. The ceramic electrolyte prevents dendrite penetration.
