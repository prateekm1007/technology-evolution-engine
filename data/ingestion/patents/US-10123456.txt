TITLE: Radiative cooling metamaterial with selectively emissive coating

ABSTRACT:
A radiative cooling structure comprising a selectively emissive layer deposited on a polymer substrate, wherein the selectively emissive layer has emissivity greater than 0.9 in the wavelength range of 8-13 microns. The structure includes a thermal insulation layer and a reflective silver backing. A heat exchanger is thermally coupled to the substrate for transferring heat away. The coating comprises a polymer film with embedded ceramic particles. The manufacturing process requires vacuum deposition of the coating onto the substrate at controlled temperature. A sensor monitors the surface temperature.
