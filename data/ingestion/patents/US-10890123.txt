TITLE: Vertical farming system with LED spectral control and hydroponic circulation

ABSTRACT:
A vertical farming system comprising multi-tier growing racks with LED lighting panels and a hydroponic nutrient circulation system. The system includes a pump for circulating nutrient solution through the growing channels. A sensor array monitors temperature, humidity, and pH. The controller adjusts LED spectra based on crop growth stage. The manufacturing process involves extrusion of polymer growing channels and assembly of LED panels. The system achieves 10 times the yield per square meter compared to conventional agriculture. The polymer substrate supports root growth.
