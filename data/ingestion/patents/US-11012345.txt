TITLE: Biodegradable polymer blend with controlled degradation rate for packaging

ABSTRACT:
A biodegradable polymer blend comprising polylactic acid and polyhydroxybutyrate for packaging applications. The blend includes a ceramic nanofiller for mechanical reinforcement. The manufacturing process involves twin-screw extrusion of the polymer blend at 180 degrees. The material achieves tensile strength comparable to polyethylene terephthalate. The polymer substrate degrades within 6 months in marine environments. The manufacturing cost is 3 times higher than conventional packaging. The material requires controlled humidity storage.
