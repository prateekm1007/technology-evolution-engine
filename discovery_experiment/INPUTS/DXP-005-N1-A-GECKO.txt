Gecko Adhesion Mechanism

Gecko toe pads have millions of keratin setae (hair-like structures, ~100 micrometers long). Each seta branches into hundreds of spatulae (~200nm). The adhesion mechanism is van der Waals forces between the spatulae and the surface. The hierarchical structure (lamella → seta → spatula) maximizes contact area. The key feature is dry adhesion — no chemical bonding, no wet adhesive, just intermolecular forces amplified by enormous contact area.

Adhesion strength: ~10 N/cm2. Reversible: the gecko peels its toes at an angle to break the van der Waals interaction.
