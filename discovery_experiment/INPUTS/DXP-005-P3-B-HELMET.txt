Sports Helmet Concussion Reduction Design

Problem: Current sports helmets use foam padding (EPS or EPP) that compresses under impact. This reduces peak force but does not distribute force path-length. Concussions still occur at impact forces well below the helmets rated protection level because the brain experiences rotational acceleration even when linear acceleration is managed.

Constraints: Must weigh under 400g. Must fit standard helmet shell. Must survive multiple impacts. Must not exceed 25mm thickness. Cost under 0. Must pass NOCSAE certification.

Need: A force-distribution mechanism that reduces peak brain acceleration by at least 20% compared to foam-only helmets, particularly for oblique (off-axis) impacts.
