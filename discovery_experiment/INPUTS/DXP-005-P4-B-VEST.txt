Bulletproof Vest Energy Absorption Improvement

Current vests use Kevlar fibers (tensile strength 3.6 GPa, toughness ~50 MJ/m3). The mechanism is fiber fracture and delamination. Need: a material or structure that absorbs 30%+ more energy per unit weight. Constraints: flexible, lightweight (<5 kg/m2), stable at -20 to 60C, 10-year shelf life.
