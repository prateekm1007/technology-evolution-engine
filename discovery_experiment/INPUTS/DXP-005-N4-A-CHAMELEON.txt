Chameleon Color Change Mechanism

Chameleons change color using structural coloration. Their skin contains two layers of iridophore cells with guanine nanocrystals. By adjusting the spacing between nanocrystals (through cellular deformation), they shift the wavelength of reflected light. This is passive reflection — no light is generated. The nanocrystal spacing (100-200nm) selects which wavelengths are constructively reflected.
