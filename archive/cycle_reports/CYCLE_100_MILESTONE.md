## What works

1. **The reaudit catches F-063-class errors structurally.** EXP-BLIND-001 was overturned without the CEO having to do it manually. The world audit independently found the FICP bridge papers.
2. **The F-064 broad-term pre-check prevents wasted blind tests.** 15 of 16 candidate pairs were rejected in cycle 90 because the bridge was already published.
3. **The non-triviality check distinguishes specific from generic mechanisms.** "selective_permeability" is specific; "contact_angle" is generic. The SPECIFIC_QUALIFIERS list handles compound terms.
4. **The F-065 full-PDF verification grounds extractions in source text.** 3 discoveries confirmed via arxiv PDF text (398K chars for EXP-BLIND-003).
5. **The intervention proposal module turns discoveries into testable hypotheses.** 3 proposals ready for a human collaborator ($195-$500, 7-14 days each).

## What doesn't work (yet)

1. **Phase VI (Laboratory Closure) is at 10%.** No experiment has been executed. The 3 intervention proposals await a human collaborator. The system can propose; it cannot execute.
2. **Calibration data is insufficient.** DR-36 requires ≥20 re-audited claims for ECE/Brier. Currently at 7. Need ~13 more reaudit cycles.
3. **The world audit uses the same search engine (z-ai) as the original extraction.** A truly independent audit would use a different search engine. The vocabulary is different, but the infrastructure is the same.
4. **arxiv coverage is limited to physics/CS.** Biology/music topics (EXP-BLIND-040: pangolin → violin bow) cannot be F-065 verified via arxiv. Need PubMed/JSTOR integration.
5. **The adversarial verification checks the audit trail + web, but doesn't run new BACON fits or dimensional analysis.** A full adversarial reaudit would re-derive laws independently.

## The path to 9/10

The CEO's mandate: "do not stop till we reach 9/10 in every benchmark."

### Blind tests: 46/100 (46%)
**Path to 9/10:** Run 54 more blind tests. At 3 per cycle, that's 18 cycles. Each cycle also grows the reaudit pool.

### Novel hits: 3/25 (12%)
**Path to 9/10:** The F-064 pre-check rejects ~80% of candidates (the easy bridges are published). Finding 22 more novel hits requires either (a) literatures with non-obvious shared intermediates, or (b) a larger entity extraction that captures more of the causal graph per literature. The honest reality: novel discoveries are rare. The system finds ~1 per 15 blind tests.

### Retrievals: 7/25 (28%)
**Path to 9/10:** Retrievals grow naturally as blind tests grow. The F-064 pre-check catches most retrievals before they waste a blind test, but some still get through.

### Null results: 32/50 (64%)
**Path to 9/10:** Nulls grow naturally. The system is honest about abstaining — 73% null rate is evidence of integrity, not failure.

### Confirmed on re-audit: 3/25 (12%)
**Path to 9/10:** Grows with novel hits. Every novel hit that survives reaudit becomes "confirmed." The world audit makes this gate meaningful — a claim that passes trail audit but fails world audit is not confirmed.

### Closed loops: 10/1000 (1%)
**Path to 9/10:** This is the hardest metric. Closed loops require physical experiments. The 3 intervention proposals are ready, but require a human collaborator with a wet/materials lab. Without execution, this metric stays at 1%.

### Verified mechanisms: 14%/75%
**Path to 9/10:** Requires Phase IV (Mechanism Induction) — structured mechanism representation. Currently at 25%. The BACON engine discovers laws but doesn't verify mechanisms.
