# WORKSTREAMS

Four parallel tracks for contact with reality.

No new laws. No new validators. No new abstractions.
Just evidence.

---

## Workstream A - Patents

Goal: Determine whether the system produces outputs that experts consider valuable.

Target: 100 patents, 10 domains, 3 reviewers per patent.

Metrics: usefulness, novelty, feasibility, implementation quality, adjacency quality.

Wedge: Upload a patent and discover everything it could become.

---

## Workstream B - Consumers

Goal: Determine whether the system helps ordinary people.

Target: 500 prompts, 50 users, 10 domains.

Metrics: satisfaction, clarity, completion rate, prototype success rate.

---

## Workstream C - Venture Discovery

Goal: Determine whether the system identifies opportunities earlier than the market.

Domains: robotics, materials, biology, manufacturing, logistics, energy.

Questions: What should exist? Why doesn't it? What changed? What is missing?

---

## Workstream D - Failure Analysis

Goal: Teach the institution to learn from dead ends.

Subjects: Segway, Google Glass, Concorde, Theranos, Quibi, Iridium, Betamax, HD-DVD, airships.

Questions: What failed? Why? What assumptions broke? What constraints moved?

---

## Primary Wedge

> Upload a patent and discover everything it could become.

This is the first product. Not all three businesses simultaneously.

## Discovery Delta (DD)

DD = discovered_value_with_system - discovered_value_without_system

If DD approaches zero, the system is merely a search engine.
If DD becomes large, the system is genuinely generative.
